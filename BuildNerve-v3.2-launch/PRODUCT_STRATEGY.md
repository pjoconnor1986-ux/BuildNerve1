# GroundAgent Product Direction

## North star
Make the best construction decision-support and operating app for UK contractors: one place that tells every role what needs attention, why it matters, what evidence supports it, and who should act.

## Beachhead
Groundworks and civil engineering subcontractors, because the workflow is operationally intense and commercially sensitive: labour, plant, materials, utilities, excavation, QA, programme and change all interact daily.

## Expansion
Once the core is strong, add configurable vertical packs for general contractors, housebuilders, concrete/frame, utilities, surfacing, landscaping and other specialist contractors.

## Product principles
- Site-first mobile experience
- Capture information once, reuse it everywhere
- AI recommendations must be traceable to records
- No AI-only safety authorisations
- Commercial value from operational evidence
- Cross-project company intelligence for directors
- Fast onboarding for SMEs
- Integrate with existing systems rather than forcing rip-and-replace

## Highest-value differentiators
1. Director intervention board across all projects
2. Site diary automatically feeding commercial/change records
3. Procurement demand generated from programme and site progress
4. Drawing/spec revision agent that explains what changed
5. Groundworks permit/readiness workflows
6. Automatic evidence packs for variations and delay events
7. Tender-to-project handover with assumptions preserved
8. Role-specific daily briefings
