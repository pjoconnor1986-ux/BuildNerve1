# BuildNerve v3.2 Build Notes

## Product principle
The app should feel faster than WhatsApp and more useful than a spreadsheet. The default interaction is: **tell BuildNerve what happened once; let the system prepare the downstream admin and actions.**

## Agentic UX
- Observe: site updates, diaries, documents, actions, project data
- Reason: connect programme, procurement, quality, safety and commercial impact
- Prepare: draft records and proposed actions
- Escalate: send only material issues to the right role
- Learn: use accepted, rejected and closed proposals as future ranking signals

## Human approval model
Low-risk admin can eventually auto-save. Medium-risk operational changes should be proposed for confirmation. Safety-critical approvals and binding commercial commitments require authorised human action.

## v3.3 target
Real persisted Quick Capture, project-specific mobile home screens, photo/voice capture, offline sync, document revision ingestion with citations, and automatic evidence packs for change/delay events.
